## 1. What is UnifAI in Simple Words?

Imagine running an office with 500 employees. Every day, your employees need to use AI (like ChatGPT) to do their work faster. But:
1. **You don't want company secrets or customer bank details leaked** on the internet.
2. **You don't want your company credit card drained** by employees asking silly or uncontrolled questions to expensive AI models.
3. **You don't want your business to freeze** if one AI company (like OpenAI) suffers an outage.

**UnifAI is the "Smart Manager + Security Guard + Finance Accountant" that sits between your company and AI tools.**

## 2. Real-World Analogies: What Each Part Actually Does

### 1. Customer (The Entire Company Building)
* **Real-World Analogy:** Your main company building (e.g. "Tata Motors" or "ClientCorp").
* **What Work It Does:** It is the parent boundary. All departments, laptops, and bills belong to this company. Another competitor company cannot see your data.

### 2. Teams (Company Departments)
* **Real-World Analogy:** Floors in your office (2nd floor Finance, 3rd floor Engineering, 4th floor HR).
* **What Work It Does:** Instead of giving 500 individual employees separate budgets, you give the "Finance Team" a shared monthly budget of $500. All finance staff use AI within that team budget.

### 3. Users (Employees with ID Badges)
* **Real-World Analogy:** Your office staff (Ramesh, Priya, John).
* **What Work It Does:** Every employee gets an official login. You can set them as an Admin (can change settings), Manager, or regular Member (can only use AI). If an employee leaves the company, you disable their ID in 1 click.

### 4. Virtual Keys (Prepaid Metro Cards instead of Master Credit Card)
* **Real-World Analogy:** Giving an employee a prepaid metro card loaded with ₹2,000 instead of handing them your master corporate credit card.
* **What Work It Does:** You never give developers the real OpenAI password or master card! You give them a "Virtual Key" (`vk-live-...`). If that key is lost or leaked, you delete it instantly. Your master account and bank account are 100% safe.

### 5. Model Providers (AI Supermarkets)
* **Real-World Analogy:** The wholesale suppliers your company buys from (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude).
* **What Work It Does:** A secure vault where the company's real supplier accounts are stored safely behind lock and key.

### 6. Model Catalog (The Restaurant Menu Card)
* **Real-World Analogy:** The menu in a company cafeteria.
* **What Work It Does:** Shows all available AI models. The business manager can simply turn OFF ultra-expensive models with a toggle switch so employees don't order a $50-per-dish AI when a $1 meal is enough.

### 7. Complexity Router (The Smart Hospital Receptionist)
* **Real-World Analogy:** Triage receptionist at a clinic.
* **What Work It Does:**
  - If a patient has a mild cold, the receptionist sends them to a junior doctor (cheap ₹100 fee).
  - If a patient needs complex brain surgery, they send them to the super-specialist surgeon (₹5,000 fee).
  - **In UnifAI:** If an employee asks: *"Hi, fix this spelling mistake"*, it routes to a tiny cheap model ($0.0001). If they ask: *"Analyze this 50-page legal contract"*, it routes to a heavy flagship model. **This saves 70% to 80% on monthly AI bills.**

### 8. Routing Rules (The Emergency Stepney / Bypass Road)
* **Real-World Analogy:** Having a spare tyre in your car or a backup generator in your office.
* **What Work It Does:** If OpenAI server crashes or goes down in the US, UnifAI automatically redirects your employee's prompt to Google Gemini or Anthropic without any error. The employee doesn't even notice the outage.

### 9. Circuit Breaker (The House Fuse / MCB Switch)
* **Real-World Analogy:** The automatic electrical fuse box in your home.
* **What Work It Does:** If an AI provider starts failing repeatedly (3 errors in a row), the system trips the fuse to "OPEN" and stops knocking on that broken door. It immediately routes everyone to the backup provider so users don't wait 60 seconds looking at a frozen loading circle.

### 10. Budgets & Limits (Prepaid Mobile Recharge)
* **Real-World Analogy:** When your monthly 1.5 GB mobile data pack runs out, internet stops automatically.
* **What Work It Does:** You set a limit: *"Team Marketing cannot spend more than $300 this month"*. When $300 is reached, it politely stops further requests. Nobody can accidentally leave a script running and bring a surprise $10,000 bill.

### 11. Pricing Overrides (Wholesale Negotiated Price)
* **Real-World Analogy:** Retail price in a shop is ₹100, but your company has a corporate contract for ₹60.
* **What Work It Does:** Shows your actual negotiated cost on company reports instead of standard retail shop prices.

### 12. Model Settings (Default Office Thermostat)
* **Real-World Analogy:** Locking the office AC thermostat at 24°C so people don't fight over the remote.
* **What Work It Does:** Sets standard company rules for AI (how creative it should be, how long answers can be, how quickly it must respond).

### 13. Browser Guard & YP-Raksha (Office Gate Security Guard)
* **Real-World Analogy:** A security guard at the office exit scanning bags and metal detectors.
* **What Work It Does:** Installed on employee laptops. When an employee opens public ChatGPT or Claude in Chrome and tries to paste:
  - Customer Credit Card numbers
  - Bank account details
  - Employee Aadhaar / Social Security numbers
  - Company source code & private passwords
  The security guard intercepts it in real-time, displays a **Red Alert**, and blocks the transmission!

### 14. Audit Logs & Dashboard (CCTV Cameras + Bank Passbook)
* **Real-World Analogy:** CCTV recording room and accounts ledger.
* **What Work It Does:** Managers can see everything: Who asked what, which department spent how much, which model was used, and if anyone tried to leak confidential information.

## 4. Supported Browsers & Dual-Layer Protection

1. **Layer 1: In-Browser Extension (`YP-Raksha`):** Directly hooks into the webpage DOM, halting transmission before the user's keystrokes leave the browser.
2. **Layer 2: System PAC Proxy:** Operating system-level fallback ensuring that even if an employee uses an un-managed browser, network packets are routed through the local Guard proxy (`127.0.0.1:18195`).

## 5. Extension Packaging Formats & Anti-Tamper Features

The extension is packaged in multiple enterprise-standard formats to ensure seamless compatibility:

* **1. Chromium Packed `.crx` (`guard_extension.crx`):** Cryptographically signed with a 2048-bit RSA private key (`guard_extension.pem`). Produces the deterministic enterprise ID: `mipfhcmlcjfhmgldkokdckhndkebkllj`.
* **2. Mozilla Firefox `.xpi` (`yp-raksha.xpi`):** Formatted for Firefox's native add-on system and loaded automatically via Firefox enterprise distributions.
* **3. Shared Unpacked Folder (`guard_extension/`):** Deployed to `C:\ProgramData\Gateway\Guard\guard_extension` without user profile path dependencies, ensuring multi-user terminal server compatibility.
* **4. Google Update Protocol (`update.xml`):** Served locally at `http://127.0.0.1:18195/extension/update.xml` to automatically trigger silent background updates whenever new security rules are deployed.
* **5. Incognito / Private Browsing Support:** Configured with `"incognito": "spanning"`. Even if an employee opens an Incognito or InPrivate window to bypass rules, DLP scanning remains active.
* **6. Anti-Tamper Lock:** Force-installed via Administrative Machine Policies (`HKLM`). Regular users cannot disable, pause, or uninstall the extension from browser settings.
